## Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and follow EU rules (GDPR). Here's how we handle your data:

### What We Collect
- Your email, username, and password (securely stored).
- Info you put in your profile.
- Listings you create (title, photos, condition, etc.).
- Messages and comments you send.
- Technical stuff (IP address, device type, etc.).

### Why We Collect It
- So the site works (e.g., logging in, showing your listings).
- To keep the platform safe (e.g., detect spam or abuse).
- To help with support requests.
- To improve the site (e.g., fix bugs or understand what people use).

### Who Sees Your Data
- **Other users** see your listings, username, and public comments.
- **We** (the admins) can see your account info and messages if needed to handle problems.
- **Our partners** (like Supabase and Vercel) help run the site and store data – they follow strict rules.
- **Nobody else.** We don’t sell your data.

### Your Rights
- See what we have about you.
- Ask us to fix or delete your info.
- Say no to certain uses (like optional cookies).
- Delete your account anytime.
